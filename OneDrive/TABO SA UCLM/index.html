Culture of Panay Bukidnon

The Panay Bukidnon are the indigenous peoples of the mountainous regions of Panay Island. Known for their vibrant Panubok embroidery, epic chants (Sugidanon), and deep reverence for nature, their culture is a living testament to pre-colonial Philippine heritage.

Theme of Tabo sa UCLM

For this Buwan ng Wika, the CTE department proudly presents a culinary journey into the heart of the highlands. Every dish served here is a story of survival, animistic harmony, and the rich bounty of the mountains.

Background & History

Long before the coastal trade flourished, the mountain rivers provided life. Our menu honors the traditional zero-waste cooking methods of the Bukidnon—using bamboo tubes, banana leaves, and natural highland fermentation.
